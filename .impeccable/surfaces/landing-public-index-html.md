# Spaceport landing page

Scope: `landing/public/index.html`, the public page at
`https://2389-research.github.io/spaceport/`. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: developers who run coding agents and shells in terminals. Action:
download the signed macOS preview. Proof: an illustrated Spaceport window
(labeled as an illustration), real `spaceport` commands from `docs/cli.md`,
and a plain ledger of what Spaceport does and what Herdr does. Constraints:
no Herdr capability presented as Spaceport's; preview status stated plainly;
no current screenshots exist.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one 1970s space-art paperback, front cover to copyright
page. It refuses the dark developer-tool page with a centered headline over a
glowing screenshot.

OWN-WORLD: Flame-orange cover stock, nebula-navy ink, cream paper, rocket red
for the one sticker. Wide Michroma title, Barlow Condensed publisher bands
and codes, Spectral blurbs. Parts: publisher band, sticker
button, boxed cover painting, ruled ledger, copyright page.

STORY: Visitor sees a native app for terminals and agent chats, learns that
Herdr runs the sessions and Spaceport is the window, then downloads the
preview.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Orange cover. Navy publisher band with nav on top. SPACEPORT
spans the full width in navy. Below, the boxed rocket painting sits center,
flanked left by the one-line pitch and right by the edition facts. A round
red "Download the preview" sticker overlaps the painting's lower-left corner.

FORM: sci-fi paperback, position 5 of the second hand. seed:ea73e4540ede449e
