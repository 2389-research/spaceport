Spaceport Mobile embeds libghostty-spm 2.2.2026111701 and DisplayLink 3.0.1.
The Ghostty source revision is 5ebe501a63425b13d9952e8c54682c046386e5af.
The Swift package applies its Apple platform patches to that revision.

The component folders contain their original copyright and license notices.
The JetBrains Mono, Symbols Nerd Font, Inconsolata and Kawkab Mono folders
cover the five font files in the iOS library. The two JetBrains Mono files
are the upright and italic variable faces. Nerd Fonts combines several
glyph collections. Its folder includes the contributing projects' notices.

Ghostty's build manifests identify the source archives for these components.
